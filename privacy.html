Privacy policy for Calendar Mirror

Last updated 22 October 2026

  • The app is a personal tool used only by its developer.
  • It uses Google Calendar only to create, update and remove events in the developer's own calendar.
  • The app runs on the developer's own computer and sends data nowhere except to that calendar.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
